GTA6 Clipbot

GTA6 Clipbot is a private, single-user tool operated by the owner of the GTA6 Clips social media accounts. It automatically creates short vertical highlight clips from GTA 6 livestreams and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, always crediting the original streamer.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not collect data from other users.
