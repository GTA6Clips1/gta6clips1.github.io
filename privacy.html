Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

GTA6 Clipbot ("the app") is a private tool used only by its owner to upload short video clips to the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts. The app has no other users.

What data the app accesses

  • OAuth access tokens for the owner's own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts, used only to upload videos and read the basic account information needed for uploading.
  • Public livestream video and public chat messages from Twitch, used only to detect highlight moments and create clips.

How data is stored

All tokens and files are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Video content and captions are sent to the respective platform only when publishing a clip.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok and Instagram

Use of TikTok and Instagram APIs is subject to the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Instagram Privacy Policy.

Deletion

The owner can delete all stored tokens and data at any time by removing them from the local computer and revoking access in the platform settings.
